# Produal stand live

Lead capture for the Produal stand at the Smart Buildings Show 2026 (ExCeL London, 14 to 15 October), with the live sensor and airflow front page and breath sensing.

Breath sensing: tap the CO2 reading on the front page and allow the microphone. Blowing gently at the bottom of the screen raises the CO2 and temperature readings a little. Sound is analysed on the device only; nothing is recorded or sent.

Three areas:

- Visitor sign-up: the front page, then a one-question-at-a-time sign-up visitors fill in themselves.
- Staff notes (PIN): the team adds a rating, next steps and notes to each visitor.
- Follow-up (PIN): counts, breakdowns, follow-up status, drafted follow-up emails, webinar list and CSV export.

Leads are stored in the browser on each device only. Export the CSV at the end of each day.
